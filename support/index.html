Frequently asked questions

How do I delete my account? What happens to my data?

Account deletion is simple and immediate: follow these steps inside the app:

  1. Open Stocky and tap your profile picture at the top right corner of the screen.
  2. Select "Personal Details" from the menu.
  3. Scroll down to the bottom and tap the red "Delete Account" button.
  4. Confirm your choice — the process starts right away.

Immediate anonymisation: As soon as you confirm deletion, all your personal data is anonymised. This means your information is permanently disconnected from your identity and cannot be recovered — even by you.

After anonymisation, the data is kept in an anonymous form for 6 months for internal analytics and legal purposes. After that period, it is fully and permanently erased from our servers.

Total process: instant anonymisation → permanent deletion after 6 months.

How do I scan a barcode to sell a product?

From the home screen, tap the barcode icon in the bottom toolbar (or the floating scan button). Point your camera at the product barcode — the product page opens instantly. Then tap "Sell" to decrement stock. The app supports EAN‑13, UPC‑A, and Code 128.

I entered the wrong stock count. How do I correct it?

Go to the product details (tap on the product from the list). Tap the edit icon (pencil) next to the stock number. You can manually adjust the quantity. There's also an "Adjust stock" option to add or subtract without overwriting history.

Is Stocky really free? What does "Freemium" mean?

Stocky is free to download and use for up to 50 products and basic statistics. If you need unlimited products, advanced reports, or multi‑user access, there is an optional subscription (monthly/yearly). All payments are handled by the app stores.

Does Stocky work for hardware store items like screws or tools?

Absolutely. You can add any product, including hardware items. Use the barcode on the package, or manually enter products without a barcode. Stocky is designed for any kind of inventory — from pasta to power drills.

How can I get a copy of my data?

Currently, Stocky does not support automated data export. If you need a copy of your personal data or stock list, please email us at support@turnaworks.com and we'll assist you manually within a reasonable timeframe.
